I am a senior applied scientist at Amazon, Berlin. I work in three areas:

- **Search agents & LLMs.** I'm building search agents end to end, from search tools and harness optimization to fine-tuning LLMs with agentic RL. I also helped build the [auto-optimize](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/opensearch-service/latest/developerguide/serverless-auto-optimize.html) feature in Amazon OpenSearch Service.
- **DevOps & forecasting.** I worked on inferring application topology from time-series and AWS configuration data, and helped build [Chronos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.07815), a foundation model for time-series forecasting.
- **Hyperparameter optimization & AutoML.** My coauthors and I won the [Best Paper Award](https://2022.automl.cc/index.html%3Fp=851.html) at the first AutoML Conference (2022). We shipped features on AWS SageMaker, including [Automatic Model Tuning](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/automatic-model-tuning.html) and [AutoPilot](https://aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/autopilot/).

Before Amazon, I earned my PhD at [Aalto University](https://www.aalto.fi/en/department-of-computer-science) under [Prof. Juho Rousu](https://people.aalto.fi/juho.rousu). We built machine-learning methods for small-molecule identification, and our method (Team Brouard) won the [CASMI challenge 2016](http://www.casmi-contest.org/2016/results.shtml) (Category 2). My thesis was named the [best Finnish bioinformatics PhD thesis of 2016–2017](https://research.aalto.fi/en/prizes/phd-thesis-award/).
